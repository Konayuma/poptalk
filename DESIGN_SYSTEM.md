# Design System: Pop Art Theme

## 1. Color Palette
- **Comic Yellow:** `#FFE600`
- **Punch Magenta:** `#FF0055`
- **Electric Cyan:** `#00E5FF`
- **Ink Black:** `#111111`
- **Paper White:** `#FAFAFA`

## 2. Typography
- **Headings & Callouts:** Heavy display comic font (e.g., `Bangers`, `Impact`, or `Bebas Neue`).
- **Body & Controls:** Monospace or bold sans-serif (e.g., `Courier Prime` or `Inter` extra-bold).

## 3. Visual Styling Rules
- **Borders:** Ultra-thick borders (`border-4 border-black` or `border-8 border-black`).
- **Shadows:** Hard offset shadows (`box-shadow: 6px 6px 0px #111111`). No smooth blurs!
- **Patterns:** Radial halftone dot patterns for backgrounds.
- **Speech Bubbles:** Jagged, explosive SVG backgrounds for status text.
